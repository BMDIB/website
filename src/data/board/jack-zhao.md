I am a proud father of two children who have both been part of the Mandarin Immersion (MI) program. My son graduated from elementary school, and my daughter is now in 5th grade. Both have participated in annual Lion Dancing for school and district awards celebrations, giving them the opportunity to share our culture with the community.

I serve as the Vice President of Events, helping organize Mid-Autumn Festival, Lunar New Year, and the Lunar New Year Teacher’s Luncheon. I enjoy sharing Asian culture with students, teachers, and staff through activities, traditions, performances, and food.

I often joke that I am the school’s “Jack of All Trades”—from hanging decorations high above the school and dressing as Cai Shen (God of Wealth) to cooking special dishes for the teachers’ luncheon. I am happy to help wherever I am needed.
