My journey with this community began in 2015 as one of the founding members who advocated and laid the groundwork to bring a Mandarin Dual Immersion program to Berryessa. Since the program officially launched in 2018, it has been an absolute joy to watch it grow into the vibrant educational community it is today. As a parent with two children currently in the program, my dedication to its success is both professional and personal. Beyond the MDI Boosters, I serve actively across various local education, civic, and youth organizations, focusing on equal rights of education, youth leadership and community advocacy.
