Our lantern decorating contest was a huge success!

More than 200 lanterns were submitted by students from Ruskin and Sierramont, filling the Ruskin multipurpose room with colorful lanterns throughout October.

Congratulations to the winners in each age group, who received a $10 Tastea gift card!

Thank you to all the students and families who participated and helped make this celebration so special.
